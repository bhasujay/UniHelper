About UniHelper

UniHelper is a web-based educational support platform developed to assist Sri Lankan students who have completed the G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L) examination and are awaiting university admission, as well as current undergraduates studying in local universities.

The platform is designed to reduce the challenges faced during university selection and to promote collaboration among students across different institutions.

Project Background

In Sri Lanka, students often rely on the printed University Admission Handbook to determine their eligibility for degree programs. This process can be manual, time-consuming, and confusing, especially for students trying to make the right academic choice.

Smart Z-Score Support

UniHelper addresses this challenge through a Z-score eligibility engine that helps students identify suitable degree programs based on district, subject stream, and Z-score. This makes the decision-making process faster and more informed.

University Applicant Module

This module supports school leavers and applicants by helping them discover degree opportunities, review options, and better understand possible university pathways.

Undergraduate Community Module

UniHelper creates a collaborative academic environment where applicants and undergraduates can connect through public Q&A discussions and peer learning sessions, commonly known as Kuppi.

University Admin Module

Verified university representatives can publish official announcements, promote degree programs, and share updates on workshops, insight days, seminars, and other academic events.

System Administrator Module

The system administrator manages core academic data, approves role applications, oversees users, and maintains the overall quality, security, and reliability of the platform.

Moderation and Integrity

Selected undergraduates act as Platform Moderators to monitor activity, flag inappropriate content, and keep discussions respectful, helpful, and informative for all users.

Technology Behind UniHelper

UniHelper is built using MVC architecture with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the frontend, PHP for backend processing, MySQL for data management, and a Python-based component for Z-score calculation.

Overall Vision

UniHelper aims to build a student-centered digital ecosystem that bridges the gap between applicants, undergraduates, and universities by combining guidance, collaboration, and centralized access to reliable information.
